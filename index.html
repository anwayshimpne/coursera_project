01 / THE PERSON BEHIND THE PURPOSE

About Me

A little about my path into plant-powered wellness.

I’m the founder and curator of Prakriti Ayurveda, a welcoming Ayurvedic medicines store inspired by the belief that well-being begins with listening—to our bodies, our surroundings, and time-tested traditions.

My goal is to make the world of Ayurveda easier to explore. I bring care to the way we select herbs, explain products, and help customers find reliable information. I value transparency, responsible sourcing, and the reminder that traditional wellness is personal—not one-size-fits-all.

Our store shares educational information and wellness products; it does not replace advice from a qualified healthcare professional.
